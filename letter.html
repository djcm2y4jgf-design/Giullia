A letter to Lando

Dear Lando,

Watching you take your first win in Miami and then win at home at Silverstone made those race Sundays ones I’ll always remember.

Thank you for being open about the tough days and for letting us share in the joy of the good ones. Seeing you become world champion made every lap of that final race worth it.

I’ll be cheering for you from lights out to the chequered flag.

Love, giullia
