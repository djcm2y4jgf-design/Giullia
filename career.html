The career

Made for
the grid.

Five milestones from Norris’s rise through the junior ranks to the Formula 1 world title.

  1. 2017

    European Formula 3 champion

    Norris won the European Formula 3 title in his first full season in the series.

  2. 2019

    Formula 1 debut with McLaren

    He made his debut at the Australian Grand Prix and scored his first points at the next race in Bahrain.

  3. 2020

    First podium in Austria

    A fastest lap on the final lap helped him secure third place at the Austrian Grand Prix.

  4. 2024

    First win in Miami

    Norris earned his first Grand Prix victory in Miami and helped McLaren win its first constructors’ title since 1998.

  5. 2025

    British Grand Prix winner and world champion

    He won at Silverstone and clinched his first Formula 1 drivers’ championship at the Abu Dhabi finale.
