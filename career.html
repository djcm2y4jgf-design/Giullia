The career

Made for
the grid.

From a rookie podium in Melbourne to seven world championships, here are a few moments that tell the story.

  1. 2007

    A rookie on the podium

    Hamilton finished third on his Formula 1 debut in Australia and won his first Grand Prix in Canada that same season.

  2. 2008

    The first world title

    At the final race in Brazil, a late pass secured the points he needed to become world champion with McLaren.

  3. 2014–20

    Six titles with Mercedes

    Hamilton won six championships in seven seasons, matching Michael Schumacher’s record of seven world titles in 2020.

  4. 2024

    A home win at Silverstone

    In his final season with Mercedes, he won the British Grand Prix for the ninth time.

  5. 2025

    A new chapter in red

    Hamilton joined Scuderia Ferrari, bringing his signature number 44 to the Italian team.
